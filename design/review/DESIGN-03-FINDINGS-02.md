### Verdict

**Close, but not ready to freeze.** Revision 02 is a substantially better document than the draft. Fourteen of seventeen findings are closed outright, three are closed in their blocking element, and none is re-opened. More importantly, the two dispositions that mattered most were closed by taking the harder path rather than the convenient one: `HD-INV-3` is left intact and the frozen §4 row is amended instead, and the provisioning events are classed `DECISION` rather than promoting a grants-free decision to `AUTHORITY`. The §9 delta list is now specific, `file:line`-cited, and — with the exceptions below — accurate about what it changes. `PP-1`'s retreat from "already exists" to "requires and specifies none" is the right correction, and `PP-8`'s explicit withdrawal of its own earlier argument is the mark of a document being written honestly rather than defended.

Three things block a freeze, and all three are narrow. The `AUTO` verdict on the amended clearing row is the only safety-relevant verdict change in the amendment and it is justified by a frozen row that says `CONFIRM` (`DESIGN-03-018`). The atomic-creation account needs one of two frozen statements named as amended and names neither, in a document whose stated compatibility promise is that every such statement is named (`DESIGN-03-019`). And the new narrowing disclosure — the mechanism that closes `-007` — is conditioned on the wrong predicate, so a mixed ceiling write walks past it (`DESIGN-03-020`). Each is a paragraph's work: change one verdict or relabel it, name one amendment, and move one condition from the row to the removal.

`DESIGN-03-021` is not DESIGN-03's to fix. The frozen authority-binding check is unsatisfiable for the first event of every project and for every kernel transition, which this review verified independently of the amendment's claim. Recommend it be recorded as a finding against `design-01-frozen` in its own right before any provisioning amendment is frozen on top of it, and that the residual scope — four frozen `HUMAN`-class rows for which `PP-10`'s replacement still fails — be decided rather than deferred, since `PP-1` leans on exactly those rows.

Recommend one more revision addressing `-018`, `-019` and `-020`, and a disposition on `-021` at the frozen-design level. A third full review should not be needed; a targeted check of the four changed passages would be enough.
