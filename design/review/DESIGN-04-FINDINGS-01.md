# DESIGN-04-FINDINGS-01

## 1. The five required resolutions

### 1. Does amendment 2 close project creation as well as the kernel case?

**No. Amendment 2 is a field note, not a creation procedure.** With amendment 1 it expresses the kernel exception to the verifier. Creation additionally depends on amendment 3, and that proposed path still fails the unchanged grant/holding rules identified below. [DESIGN-04:115](../DESIGN-04-baseline-correction.md#L115) and [:118](../DESIGN-04-baseline-correction.md#L118) overstate what amendment 2 closes. Frozen [domain-model.md:691](../domain-model.md#L691) and [:391](../domain-model.md#L391) require project references on decisions and activations respectively; [:38](../domain-model.md#L38) requires integrity, not an earlier committed project.

**A decision can exist transaction-locally before the project's lifecycle creation is published.** Allocate the identifier, prepare the prospective project, record the snapshot and authenticated decision, perform creation, and validate the complete reference graph at commit. This is the deferred-reference reasoning explicitly demonstrated for activation/run insertion in [VERIFY-01:98](DESIGN-01-FINDINGS-02-VERIFY-01.md#L98), especially [:104](DESIGN-01-FINDINGS-02-VERIFY-01.md#L104)–[:109](DESIGN-01-FINDINGS-02-VERIFY-01.md#L109), and for project/snapshot insertion at [:50](DESIGN-01-FINDINGS-02-VERIFY-01.md#L50)–[:59](DESIGN-01-FINDINGS-02-VERIFY-01.md#L59). It does not authorize an act whose authority predicates remain false.

The draft does rely on this mechanism: `AX-16` makes the decision and project durable together ([DESIGN-04:163](../DESIGN-04-baseline-correction.md#L163)); `R3.12` invokes unspecified reference-check timing ([DESIGN-04:181](../DESIGN-04-baseline-correction.md#L181)). But that basis cites the refuted half of **002**, not **006**. The explicit 006 quotation at [DESIGN-04:43](../DESIGN-04-baseline-correction.md#L43) is confined to a no-amendment discussion of activation/run references. The creation argument must explicitly connect itself to **VERIFY-01's D01-F02-006 refutation**, including deferred checks and transaction-local ordering. The reference cycle is solvable; its resolution must not be left implicit or confused with permission to defer authority checking.

There are two substantive residues. Direct exercise still violates frozen `AX-1` and the possession checks ([authority-matrix.md:12](../authority-matrix.md#L12), [:292](../authority-matrix.md#L292), [transition-matrix.md:49](../transition-matrix.md#L49)). Also, adding a creation decision adds its recording and consumption transitions: the claimed snapshot-at-0/creation-at-1 history omits them ([transition-matrix.md:393](../transition-matrix.md#L393), [:394](../transition-matrix.md#L394), [domain-model.md:797](../domain-model.md#L797)). **DESIGN-04-001 and -004.** This review does not reinstate the refuted claim that foreign keys make atomic creation impossible.

### 2. Does amendment 1 change the predicate itself?

**Yes, for the kernel verifier case.** [DESIGN-04:75](../DESIGN-04-baseline-correction.md#L75) targets frozen [evidence-model.md:176](../evidence-model.md#L176), quotes it accurately at [:80](../DESIGN-04-baseline-correction.md#L80), and replaces the universal live-grant requirement with the class-dependent predicate at [:86](../DESIGN-04-baseline-correction.md#L86). A `KERNEL` event with `actor_kind = RUNTIME` and absent `authority_ref` now satisfies that predicate. This directly addresses the objection in [D01-F02-001:20](DESIGN-01-FINDINGS-02.md#L20) and [VERIFY-01:38](DESIGN-01-FINDINGS-02-VERIFY-01.md#L38): optionality alone was insufficient.

The underlying exclusive kernel holding is frozen [authority-matrix.md:18](../authority-matrix.md#L18), [:34](../authority-matrix.md#L34), [:245](../authority-matrix.md#L245), [:266](../authority-matrix.md#L266). The absent-reference encoding is a disclosed choice. **No finding against that encoding or the replacement of :176.** This local success does not establish the draft's claim to close both halves of 001, nor exempt its new human path from other frozen enforcement points. Amendment 2 also introduces a separate optional-field regression, **DESIGN-04-012**.

### 5. Do amendments 10–12 correctly address genesis and VERIFY-01's retained residue?

**They address the correct residue.** The first event is already `seq = 0` with the specified predecessor hash ([evidence-model.md:127](../evidence-model.md#L127)); ordinary steps 2/3 read fields without initial values ([:135](../evidence-model.md#L135), [:136](../evidence-model.md#L136)). Installing the initial project/head state and selecting zero for the first reservation are disclosed refinements. They preserve event/head atomicity and post-persistence visibility ([:138](../evidence-model.md#L138), [:139](../evidence-model.md#L139)). They need not amend reference integrity, the lock, or the external-effect exclusion. This matches the narrowed residue at [VERIFY-01:59](DESIGN-01-FINDINGS-02-VERIFY-01.md#L59), not the separately refuted visibility impossibility.

**But the complete creation account is wrong after AX-14.** [DESIGN-04:535](../DESIGN-04-baseline-correction.md#L535) still fixes snapshot event 0 and creation event 1, as if adding the approval added no event. The approval must reach VALID through [transition-matrix.md:393](../transition-matrix.md#L393), consuming the already-current SNAP; every transition emits an event ([domain-model.md:797](../domain-model.md#L797)). The recording event carries `decision.record`, whereas the creating event carries `project.create`; a state-changing event cannot merge both capability identities ([domain-model.md:789](../domain-model.md#L789), [:890](../domain-model.md#L890)). Replay follows chain order ([evidence-model.md:187](../evidence-model.md#L187)). The decision then needs the separately ordered consumption event at [transition-matrix.md:394](../transition-matrix.md#L394), also required by AX-19.

A witness ordering, with unrelated authority defects held aside, is **0 snapshot → 1 decision recorded VALID → 2 project created → 3 decision consumed**, in one transaction with a final head at 3. The decision is VALID when used; consumption cites the lower-seq creation event. This is a feasible refinement, not a demand for one commit per event. The draft must account for all four transitions and stop asserting the two-event final values. **-004.**

Amendment 12 also drops the append table's third, `failure handling`, cell: its proposed line [DESIGN-04:412](../DESIGN-04-baseline-correction.md#L412) has two columns, whereas frozen [evidence-model.md:132](../evidence-model.md#L132) and [:136](../evidence-model.md#L136) have three. Preserve the `—` cell. **-013.**
