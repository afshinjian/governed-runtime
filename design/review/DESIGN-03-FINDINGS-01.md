### Verdict

**Not ready to freeze.** The document is carefully built and its §1 gap analysis is correct: the frozen design does register four `HUMAN` project-governance capabilities with no transition row, no capability for `capability_ceiling`, and no decision kind for an initial budget or a policy replacement, and a project genuinely cannot be provisioned by any specified operation. The §1.1 implementation observations were spot-checked and hold at this baseline.

What blocks a freeze is not the direction but three classes of defect. First, the document's own completeness promise fails: §9 claims that only the named deltas supersede frozen statements, while the axis-3 gate, the `AUTHORITY` class taxonomy, and the human-authored policy constraint are all extended or contradicted without being named (`DESIGN-03-002`, `DESIGN-03-005`, `DESIGN-03-009`). For an amendment whose value is precisely that a reader can bound what changed, that is disqualifying on its own. Second, two load-bearing rules are stated more broadly than their arguments support: `PP-4` unbounds human exercise of every `HUMAN`-class capability when it needs to unbound five (`DESIGN-03-001`), and the `HD-INV-3` exception is scoped by prose where a predicate is required, with a third frozen consumer of the same token left outside it by assertion (`DESIGN-03-003`). Third, §6 — the most mechanical section, and the one an implementation would follow literally — is internally unsatisfiable on the chain values and reproduces a defect §1.1 charges against the current code (`DESIGN-03-004`, `DESIGN-03-006`).

None of this requires redesign. Fourteen of the seventeen findings close by narrowing a claim, adding a citation, or moving an item from "derived" to "chosen". `DESIGN-03-003` and `DESIGN-03-004` need a specified mechanism rather than a rewording: a predicate that names which second transition a `DEC(EXTEND_BUDGET)` discharges and for which holder, and a consistent account of what can exist before a project record does. Recommend a revised DESIGN-03 and a second review before the frozen files are edited. `P-INV-2`-at-grant-time (`DESIGN-03-007`) is an acceptable reading provided the narrowing row is made to record what it did not contain; it should not freeze in its current silent form.
