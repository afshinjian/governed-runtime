## 1. Four requested resolutions

### 1.1 Does the new meaning of possession work at every frozen consumer?

**No.** The two expressly edited holding checks still exclude the new root exception. Amendment 3 calls `decision.record` an exception **to the requirement of a form** ([D04-02:168](../DESIGN-04-baseline-correction-02.md#L168)); amendments 4 and 5 nevertheless require possession **by an authority form**, enumerating the four and omitting that exception ([191](../DESIGN-04-baseline-correction-02.md#L191), [213](../DESIGN-04-baseline-correction-02.md#L213)). An authenticated human recording the first decision satisfies AX-15 but cannot satisfy those checks without the very prior decision AX-15 dispenses with. Frozen [transition-matrix.md:393](../transition-matrix.md#L393) is still a capability-bearing transition. **DESIGN-04-015; -001 is only partially closed.**

There is also a new universal prohibition: AX-15 says “no other capability is self-held by a principal” ([D04-02:436](../DESIGN-04-baseline-correction-02.md#L436)). `RUNTIME_KERNEL` is a principal explicitly permitted to grant KERNEL capabilities to itself ([authority-matrix.md:34](../authority-matrix.md#L34)); AX-7 assigns those holdings to the kernel ([18](../authority-matrix.md#L18)). Amendments 1 and 3 depend on precisely that own holding. “Not an instance of AX-7” distinguishes `decision.record` from kernel possession; it does not qualify the final universal prohibition. The prohibition needs an explicit non-KERNEL scope. **DESIGN-04-016.**

It includes set membership, grant provenance and explanations that presuppose possession, as well as literal occurrences of *hold*. ### 1.3 Does the new G-1 work for all 44 rows and all five allowed entities?
